The past is not quiet.

ARCHIVE is a digital museum of Nigerian photography, music, fashion and design. Every record here was made by someone in Lagos, Ibadan, Enugu or Kano who had something to say. Turn the dial and hear it.

1977

The year every room was in the same city.

From 15 January to 12 February 1977, Lagos hosted FESTAC '77, the Second World Black and African Festival of Arts and Culture. Artists, musicians, designers and performers came from across Africa and the diaspora. The National Theatre was built to hold it.

For four weeks, photography, music, fashion and design were not separate subjects. They were one event.
